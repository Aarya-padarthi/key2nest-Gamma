Key2Nest Home Loans

One system. Two themes.
Timeless by design.

Deep navy, 22-karat gold, and soft ivory — rendered as design tokens so every component inherits the brand automatically in both themes. This page reads the live production stylesheet; what you see here is exactly what ships. Use the toggle (top right) to flip themes — token values and contrast ratios below update in real time. Full rationale lives in DESIGN-SYSTEM.md.

01

Brand palette

Gold is used two ways: as a surface (buttons, badges — always the bright metallic ramp) and as text (deepens to #8B600A / #6E4B08 on ivory for contrast). Status hues are deliberately desaturated — sage, copper, refined red, and the teal already used as the site's cool counter-light.

03

Typography

Source Serif 4 (display & headings — optical sizing on, weight 380–500) with Source Sans 3 (body, UI, eyebrows). Scale is a Major Third (1.25) anchored at 16px: 13 · 16 · 20 · 25 · 31 · 39 · 49 · 61 · 76 · 95.

04

Elevation

Two-part shadows: a tight near shadow for edge definition plus a long, soft far shadow for depth. Navy-tinted in light mode, never gray. Gold glows are reserved for gold surfaces only.

--shadow-1 · Rest

Cards at rest, table containers

--shadow-2 · Raised

Hover states, active cards

--shadow-3 · Floating

Dropdowns, popovers, tooltips

--shadow-4 · Modal

Dialogs, team profile modal

07

Motion

Philosophy: calm confidence. Nothing bounces, nothing spins for decoration. Reveals rise 24px once on scroll; hovers lift 2–4px; numbers tween with power2.out; everything honors prefers-reduced-motion.

08

Accessibility — live contrast

Computed in-browser against the current theme. WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI. Focus rings are 2px gold (--focus-ring), offset 3px, on every interactive element.
