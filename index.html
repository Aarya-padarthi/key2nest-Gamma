We compute your original monthly payment, add your extra principal, then solve for the new payoff using n = −log(1 − P·r/M) / log(1+r) — where P is balance, r is monthly rate, M is the new monthly payment. Interest saved = total paid (original) − total paid (with extras).

The Process

Four steps. A clear path home.

  1. 01

    Discover

    An initial consultation to understand your goals, timeline, and finances. No credit is pulled at this stage — that happens later in the process, only with your authorization.

  2. 02

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions buyers actually ask.

Plain-English answers from experienced mortgage loan originators.

Which states is Key2Nest licensed in?

Key2Nest Home Loans LLC is licensed in Arizona, Arkansas, Florida, Tennessee, and Texas under NMLS #2819804. We can help with a purchase or refinance on residential property located in any of those five states. If your property sits outside them, tell us anyway — we'd rather say so plainly than waste your time.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan program. Conventional loans typically require a minimum of 3% down payment. FHA program accepts 3.5% down with a qualifying credit profile. Some programs offer low or zero down payment options for eligible buyers, and some states offer down-payment-assistance programs through their housing finance agencies. We'll walk through what fits your file in a short call.

How long does pre-approval take?

Pre-approval letters are often issued within a couple of business days once we have your documentation — income verification, credit authorization, and recent asset statements. Timing varies with the completeness of your file. A real pre-approval (not a pre-qualification) strengthens your offer in any competitive market.

What's the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

Pre-qualification is a quick informal estimate based on what you tell us. Pre-approval is a documented review of credit, income, and assets that sellers and listing agents treat as a real commitment. For competitive offers, pursue full pre-approval before submitting.

How does Key2Nest get paid?

As a mortgage broker, Key2Nest is compensated when a loan closes — either by the lender or by you, depending on how your loan is structured. Whichever applies, the amount is disclosed in writing on your Loan Estimate before you commit to anything. Federal rules also prohibit a loan originator's compensation from being tied to your interest rate or loan terms, so there is no incentive to steer you toward a costlier option. We'll walk through the actual figures on your file rather than quote generic numbers here.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

A licensed loan originator reviews your inquiry personally. We'll reach out by phone or email (your preference) to schedule an introductory conversation. No automated drip emails. No call-center handoffs. Wherever possible, the person you first speak with stays with you through closing.
